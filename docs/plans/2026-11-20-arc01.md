## Review focus
- Non-MCP HTTP service at the expected port: initialization must fail.
- Wrong editor/project: never permit scene writes.
- Duplicate registrations or quoted TOML tables: preserve or fail safely.
- SSE response streams: stop at matching response, do not wait for connection closure.
- Existing test level or unsaved content: never overwrite/discard it.
